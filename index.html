THE ARTIST BACKGROUND & PHILOSOPHY

Every frame is a decision.

I am Sikder Rakibul Islam, currently pursuing my B.Voc in Multimedia and Animation (5th Semester) at Bharathiar University.

My obsession is with the psychological friction of an edit: where audio leads the eye, where motion clarifies data, and how a documentary can turn dense information into an unforgettable human story. Whether it is deep space telemetry or socio-economic realities, I craft visual work built to leave a lasting impact.
